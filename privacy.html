Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 12, 2026

French Phrases Helper (hosted at josestudio77-creator.github.io/French-Helper/) is built as a tool for students and parents in French Immersion programs. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

1. Data Collection & Children's Privacy (COPPA)

We are fully committed to children's privacy.

  • We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
  • We do not require users to create an account.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers from our users.

2. Local Storage (The "Backpack")

All data you enter into the app—including homework lists, custom notes, custom photos, and teacher recordings—is stored exclusively on your device using browser "localStorage."

This data is never transmitted to us or any third-party servers. If you clear your browser cache or delete the app data, this information will be permanently removed.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide high-quality educational features, the app uses the following services:

  • Google Translate API: Used temporarily to translate French phrases into English. No data is stored or used for profiling by this service.
  • Device Speech Synthesis: The app uses your device's native text-to-speech engine to provide pronunciations. This happens entirely on your device.

4. Photos and Audio

If you choose to use the "Photo Crop" or "Teacher Recording" features, these files are converted into data strings and stored in your device's local memory. We have no access to your camera roll or your microphone recordings.
