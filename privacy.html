Privacy Policy for French Fun

Last Updated: March 2024

1. Introduction

French Fun (also known as "French Phrases Helper" on some platforms), available at https://josestudio77-creator.github.io/French-Helper/, is an educational tool designed for French Immersion students to practice vocabulary, dictation, and handwriting. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children.

2. Data Collection (COPPA Compliance)

We do not collect any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from our users. This app does not require an account, email address, name, or any other personal details to function. There are no analytics, tracking, or advertisements within the application.

3. Data Storage

All data entered into the app—including custom homework phrases, teacher recordings, custom photos for phrases, game scores (wins/losses), and personalized app settings (like voice speed)— is stored locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage). This means your data never leaves your device and is not uploaded to our servers or any third-party servers. You are in complete control of your data.

4. Third-Party Services

To provide its core functionalities, French Fun utilizes the following services, which are processed directly by your device and do not involve our collection of personal data:

  • Google Translate API (via web browser): Used temporarily to provide English translations for French words you enter. No personal data from your device is sent to us.
  • Device Speech Engine (Text-to-Speech): The app uses the native Android/iOS/Desktop voice engines on your device to pronounce words and phrases. Your voice data is processed locally by your device.
  • Cropper.js: A JavaScript library used for photo cropping, which operates entirely within your browser on your device.

5. Parental Control

Parents have full control over the data stored by the app. Clearing your browser's cache and site data, or uninstalling the app (if installed as a PWA), will permanently remove all stored homework, recordings, and photos from your device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
